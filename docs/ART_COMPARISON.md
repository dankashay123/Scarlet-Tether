# Hollow Saint vs Otherworld Legends

Compared 2026-10-18 against 8 Steam screenshots, 9 App Store screenshots,
15 wiki screenshots and 18 wiki sprite GIFs, 6 YouTube thumbnails and 16
Reddit posts. The reference images stay out of the repo (they are not ours).

Scale note: OWL draws its art at 4x on a 1080p screen, so its view is about
270 art pixels tall. Heroes are about 40 px tall, item icons about 26 px.
Our view was 360 px tall with a 52 px Saint (15% of the screen). Round 5
moved to a 430 px view so the Saint is about 12%, matching OWL's camera
distance, and rooms grew to 1 to 1.3 screens like OWL's.

## Differences (before this round)

| Area | Otherworld Legends | Hollow Saint (before) |
|---|---|---|
| Floors | Large, calm slabs in mixed sizes (1x1, 2x1, 2x2 of a ~32 px unit). Flat body colour, a 1 px lit top-left edge, a dark gap, a few cracks. Low contrast, so actors pop. | Small 32x16 bricks in running bond. High contrast, warm sand, reads as a brick wall and competes with the actors. Same floor in every theme. |
| Room shape | Each room is a box: walls on all four sides with lit tops, thick dark sides, black void beyond. | Only the back wall is modelled. Side and bottom edges stop at a flat dark fill with no rim. |
| Wall shadows | Walls cast a soft shadow onto the floor (top and left). | A 7 px strip under the back wall only. |
| Light | Dim base with bright warm pools around torches and candles, plus soft additive bloom on lights and effects. | Evenly lit, with a full-screen orange soft-light wash that flattens depth. |
| Colour | Floors desaturated and cool or neutral; characters, loot and effects saturated. | Floor as saturated as the Saint; little separation. |
| Effects | Big, bright effects with white-hot cores, glow and chunky shapes. Hits flash and spark. | Small, older hand-drawn effects (bolt 15 px, impact 19 px). |
| Item icons | About 26 px, sculpted, dark outline, strong highlight. | 14 px flat icons. |
| Damage numbers | Large, bold, outlined numerals. | Bold 5x7, fine at x2, small at x1. |
| UI font | Chunky, readable labels and numbers. | Thin 5x7 font, small at 360 px. |
| HUD | Framed portrait, thick bars with icons. | Similar; fine. |
| Outlines | Dark outlines on actors and props. | Same (already matched). |
| Proportions | Heads big, bold silhouettes. | Matched in v5. |

## What this round changes

1. Floors: large sculpted flagstones in mixed sizes, one material per theme
   (crypt grey, chapel warm, ossuary bone, flooded slate), low contrast.
2. Walls on every side: lit wall tops with a rim, side walls with depth,
   soft cast shadows on the floor from the top and left.
3. Light: darker ambient, no global orange wash, stronger warm pools and
   bloom around torches, candles, bolts and impacts.
4. Effects and item icons remodelled with the SDF renderer at the new size.
5. A larger, bolder UI font.

## Pixel Hunter Idle: Retro RPG (round 3)

Studied from its App Store screenshots and YouTube thumbnails (ZillionGames).

| Area | Pixel Hunter Idle | What we took |
|---|---|---|
| Characters | Very chibi heroes (head about half the body), bold flat colours, a red-hooded hunter like ours. | Kept our taller Saint (the owner asked for less chibi earlier); borrowed the readable silhouette and glowing weapon. |
