# Credits

Everything below is free to use in commercial games. CC0 needs no credit,
but we give it anyway. The audio is rebuilt from the source packs with
`tools/build-audio.sh` (trimmed, mixed, normalised and looped with ffmpeg).

## Sound effects: Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0

| Pack | Used for |
|---|---|
| [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) | hits, kills, block, hurt, execute, breakables, thurible, flask glass, chain, penance bell, gate slam, shrine and elite bells, death |
| [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) | crossbow click, execute slice, chain latch, gate door and creak, chest coins |
| [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) | UI taps, item pickup, health globe, perfect-dodge chime, shrine shimmer |
| [Casino Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio) | gold pickup |
| [Sci-Fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds) | flask splash, low booms under the elite toll and death |

The dash whoosh is synthesised in the game (filtered noise), no file.

## Music and ambience: OpenGameArt.org, CC0 1.0

| Track | Author | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| [Dark Castle - Part I](https://opengameart.org/content/dark-castle-part-i) | umplix | town music |
| [Dungeon Ambience](https://opengameart.org/content/dungeon-ambience) | yd | dungeon music |
| [Mild wind background noise](https://opengameart.org/content/mild-wind-background-noise) | bashar3a | town wind |
| [Crickets ambient noise (loopable)](https://opengameart.org/content/crickets-ambient-noise-loopable) | wolfgang | town night crickets |

Freesound and Sonniss were not needed: Freesound downloads need an account,
and the Sonniss bundles are multi-gigabyte archives.
